#### Multi-GPU and Advanced Optimization
Strata supports sophisticated multi-GPU configurations, primarily through **layer splitting** (pipeline parallelism). This allows a model to be split across multiple NVIDIA or AMD cards, where each card handles a contiguous range of layers.
*   **Layer Split:** The engine automatically or manually assigns layers to GPUs. Recent updates have optimized the "hand-off" between cards, reducing latency by launching subsequent GPU windows early and avoiding synchronous commits. This has resulted in significant speedups (e.g., +36% on dual RX 7900 XTX cards).
*   **Pipeline Windows:** An experimental feature (`--pipeline-windows`) allows cards to overlap work by guessing the next token, further increasing throughput.
*   **Expert Caching:** The engine uses an adaptive expert cache to keep frequently used "experts" (parts of the Mixture-of-Experts model) in VRAM or pinned RAM, falling back to CPU or SSD for less frequent ones. Research documents detail the trade-offs between VRAM, RAM, and SSD access speeds, noting that expert caching primarily benefits decode speed rather than prompt reading.
